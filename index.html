About

I recently relocated from Italy to Málaga to start a new chapter in an international environment.

Over the past five years, I’ve worked across industrial automation and enterprise insurance software, building backend applications, integrations, business logic, and data flows with Python, while also working with Java and Spring Boot in an enterprise environment. I enjoy understanding how different parts of a system connect and taking ownership from the initial problem through to delivery.

I care about clear service boundaries, maintainable code, reliable integrations, and testing that makes backend changes safer to ship. My recent work also includes full-stack delivery with FastAPI, Next.js, TypeScript, and Firebase.

My current independent project, Document Intelligent Hub, is a full-stack application built around a FastAPI backend and a Next.js frontend. I use it to keep developing my backend and software engineering skills while exploring how applied RAG can support useful, source-grounded tools.

Outside software, I’m involved in animal-rights activism, calisthenics, and tabletop role-playing games.
